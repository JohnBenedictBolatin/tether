# Tether anatomy asset credits\n\nThese are separate source models bundled locally so the viewer works offline. Tether's React, TypeScript, and Three.js code does not change the licenses for the anatomical data.\n\n## Body surface, skeleton, muscles, digestive structures, and major vessels\n\n**BodyParts3D 4.0**, \u00a9 The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), sourced from the official 99% reduced polygon mesh archive. Attribution: \u201cBodyParts3D, \u00a9 The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International.\u201d The official archive license page currently states CC BY 4.0 and was updated 2025-02-27: [official license](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html).\n\nThe original OBJ headers also contain a CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan notice. The archive license page currently states CC BY 4.0; both notices are recorded here because they differ. Adaptations made for Tether: selected source parts; combined them into independently toggleable systems; triangulated polygon faces; recomputed surface normals; transformed the source Z-up axis into the viewer\u2019s Y-up scene; scaled the full model uniformly; recolored surfaces; and exported a local GLB. No anatomy was generated.\n\n## Heart\n\nThe heart meshes come from OrganUI's [heart-3d](https://github.com/organui/heart-3d) explorer, using its BodyParts3D 4.0 model under CC BY 4.0. The model's source meshes remain separate for heart structure selection. See [heart-ATTRIBUTION.md](heart-ATTRIBUTION.md) for the supplied attribution and detailed preparation note.\n\n## Lungs and airways\n\nThe lung meshes come from OrganUI's [lungs-3d](https://github.com/organui/lungs-3d) explorer. That model uses selected BodyParts3D / Anatomography 4.3 structures and is licensed CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. The original models are adapted, grouped, recentered, uniformly scaled, and converted to GLB by OrganUI. See [lungs-LICENSE.txt](lungs-LICENSE.txt). Tether bundles the credited model and applies a uniform placement transform in the body scene.\n\nThe credited models are educational reference anatomy. Their colors are interface styling; they do not encode measured health status. DBCLS, NIH, and the model contributors do not endorse Tether.\n
